Forward Propagation & Activation Functions (ReLU + Sigmoid)

Input features $\mathbf{x}$ pass through dense layers where each neuron computes $z = \mathbf{w}^T\mathbf{x} + b$. Hidden neurons apply $\text{ReLU}(z)=\max(0,z)$ to model non-linear interactions, while the output neuron applies $\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}$ to output a calibrated probability $P(\text{Pass}) \in (0,1)$.

a⁽¹⁾ = max(0, W⁽¹⁾x + b⁽¹⁾)  |  ŷ = 1 / (1 + exp(−(W⁽²⁾a⁽¹⁾ + b⁽²⁾)))

Binary Cross-Entropy Loss, Backpropagation & Adam Optimizer

Prediction error is quantified by Binary Cross-Entropy. Backpropagation applies the chain rule from output to input layers to compute weight gradients $\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L}$, and Adam updates parameters using first ($m_t$) and second ($v_t$) gradient moments. Note: Distributed hidden weights do not act as simple linear coefficients.

L(y, ŷ) = −(1/N)∑[y·ln(ŷ) + (1−y)·ln(1−ŷ)]  |  θ ← θ − η·m̂_t / (√v̂_t + ε)

1. Introduction & Problem Statement

EduPredict applies Educational Data Mining (EDM) and Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) to identify students at risk of academic failure early enough for faculty advising and remedial intervention. Traditional linear cutoffs miss non-linear interactions among attendance, daily study hours, prior exam scores, assignment completion, participation, and sleep hygiene.

2. Objectives & Literature Review

Objectives: Synthesize cohorts, ingest custom CSVs, prevent data leakage via training-only preprocessing, train a TensorFlow.js feedforward ANN, evaluate ROC/threshold sensitivity, and predict individual outcomes.

Literature: Grounded in Cortez & Silva (2008) and Romero & Ventura (2010) on student performance analytics, Rumelhart et al. (1986) on backpropagation, Nair & Hinton (2010) on ReLU, and Kingma & Ba (2015) on Adam optimization.

3. Research Methodology & Leakage Prevention

Student_ID, Final_Score, and Performance are excluded from model inputs. Numerical features undergo mean imputation and Z-score standardization $(x-\mu_{\text{train}})/\sigma_{\text{train}}$; categorical features undergo mode imputation and one-hot encoding fitted strictly on the training split.

4. Limitations & Conclusion

Limitations: Synthetic cohorts follow parametric assumptions; mean/mode imputation assumes Missing At Random (MAR); severe class imbalance requires threshold tuning; over-parameterized networks can overfit small cohorts; neural weights require post-hoc sensitivity analysis rather than direct linear interpretation.

EduPredict ANN Project & Viva Examination Guide

This standalone application demonstrates the complete deep learning workflow for MBA Data Science & Artificial Intelligence:

1. Synthetic Cohort Generation & Custom CSV Upload:

Generates realistic student records (100 to 5,000 rows) across 13 academic attributes with a configurable Passing Score Cutoff (default 40), or ingests external CSV files via PapaParse with full validation.

2. Zero-Leakage Preprocessing & Genuine TensorFlow.js ANN:

Excludes Student_ID, Final_Score, and Performance from inputs, fits mean/mode imputation, Z-score standardization, and one-hot encoding strictly on the training set, and trains a feedforward neural network (ReLU hidden layer(s) + Sigmoid output) using Adam and Binary Cross-Entropy.

3. Interactive 2D Decision Surface, ROC & Threshold Analyzer:

Visualizes the learned ANN probability surface over any pair of student features, plots live training/validation loss and accuracy curves, and updates Confusion Matrix, Precision, Recall, and F1 in real time as you adjust the classification threshold $\tau$.

4. Individual Student Outcome Simulator & Exports:

Transforms individual student profiles with the exact training preprocessor and runs a live TensorFlow.js forward pass to estimate Pass/Fail probabilities.
